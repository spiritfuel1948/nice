# Sources (Researched October 2026)

The list has **47 sources**, grouped by topic. Each one is tagged by how much to
trust it:

- 🟢 **Official / primary:** Amazon KDP help pages, court rulings, peer-reviewed
  research, the original authors of a technique
- 🔵 **Expert / journalism:** established craft teachers and trade press
- 🟡 **Practitioner / vendor blog:** useful, but the writer may be selling
  something or summarizing someone else. Double-check the numbers.
