Get a better voice, and other tips
  • Keep the screen on. Most phones stop reading when the screen locks. This page asks your phone to stay awake while it reads.
  • Tap any paragraph to start reading from there.
  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Download a voice marked "Enhanced" or "Premium," then pick it in the Voice menu here.
  • Android: search Settings for "Text-to-speech" and install Google's voice data.
  • Windows: open this page in Microsoft Edge and pick a voice with "Natural" in its name. They sound the most human.
  • Menu names vary a little by device and version.
