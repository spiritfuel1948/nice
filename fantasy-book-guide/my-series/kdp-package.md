## 6. Cover (lean budget)
**Ebook cover: done in Canva (2026-10-21).** It shows a golden sword wound with a red
thread, with the bridge city at dawn behind it, the tagline, *The Bridgewatch ·
Book One*, *The Heart-Thread*, and **Luke Moreland**.
- **Open it:** [The Heart-Thread book cover in Canva](https://www.canva.com/d/B4buRotsOyVEj53)
  (design ID `DAHYpoQPBIk`, 1410 × 2250 px).
- **Download for KDP:** in Canva, **Share → Download → JPG**, quality at max, and set the
  size to **2560 px tall** (about 1604 × 2560). Upload that JPG on KDP's "Kindle eBook
  Content" page. *(Claude's cloud sandbox can't reach Canva's download server, so
  this step is yours. It takes about 1 minute.)*
- **AI disclosure:** the art is AI-generated in Canva, so tick **"AI-generated: images"** on KDP.
- **Still to do:** the **paperback full-wrap cover** (front + spine + back) to the sizes below.

**Original lean-budget advice:** a premade YA fantasy cover (~$70–150) from a human cover
designer is still an option if you want a pro look later. KDP's free **Cover
Creator** is the $0 fallback.

**Cover brief (sizes for the paperback wrap, or for a designer):**
- **Title:** The Heart-Thread · **Series line:** The Bridgewatch · Book One · **Author:** Luke Moreland
- **Genre/feel:** YA epic fantasy, classic adventure, PG-13, a hint of romance
- **Tagline:** *She was sent to kill him. Now if he dies, so does she.*
- **Image ideas:** a blazing gold sword wound with a glowing **red thread**; or two
  hands/wrists bound by a red thread; or a city on a vast bridge over a chasm
  at dawn. **Palette:** gold and crimson on deep night-blue.
- **Ebook size:** 1600 × 2560 px JPG
- **Paperback full wrap:** trim 5.5" × 8.5", **261 pages, white paper → spine 0.588"**,
  0.125" bleed → **11.838" × 8.75"** overall (3552 × 2625 px at 300 DPI).
  Leave room bottom-right of the back cover for KDP's barcode (2" × 1.2").
- If the designer uses **AI-generated art**, tick "AI-generated: images" on KDP too.
