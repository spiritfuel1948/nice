# Season 1 Outline: *The Heart-Thread*

> **Working titles** (to confirm at the end): Series **The Heart-Thread**.
> Part 1 *The Burned March* · Part 2 *The Hollowwood* · Part 3 *The Broken Oath*
> · Part 4 *The Bridge of Dawn*.
> The author asked for the whole season to be drafted (AI drafts, author reads
> at the end). All decisions below follow `story-bible.md`. New choices made
> while outlining are marked **(new)**.

---

## Style guide (keeps 32 chapters consistent)
- **Third person limited, past tense. Odd chapters are Maren, even chapters are Emrys.**
- YA, PG-13: real danger, deaths happen off-page or quickly, no gore, mild language.
- **Maren's voice:** short sentences, formal, dry. She calls him "wizard."
  She says "Emrys" for the first time in Ch 11.
- **Emrys's voice:** long playful sentences, wordplay, questions. He calls her
  "Sir Grim" (she's only a squire, which is the joke). When he stops joking,
  it's serious.
- Show emotion through body, gut, thought, and action (Lesson 8). Go easy on
  smiles, nods, and sighs.
- Lore arrives through conflict, discovery, songs, and echoes. No lectures.
- Every chapter has a goal, a conflict, and a turn, and ends on a hook or a new
  question.
- **Slow-burn:** sparks only. A hand held, a near-moment. No kiss in Season 1.
- Target: about 2,300–2,800 words per chapter, about 20k per part.

## The villain (Step 7) (new)
**Warden Isaura Vell.** **Wants:** keep the war going, because the Span's
wealth and power exist *because* of it: tolls, arms, ransoms, brokering every
truce. **Believes she's right:** "Peace between them would end with one side
destroying the other. Someone must hold the scales." **She is Maren's Lie
taken to the extreme:** order and control matter more than what's right.
**Her family secret:** 300 years ago her ancestor **Corvane Vell**, whose
ferries were the only way across the Sunder, murdered Prince Caelan and
framed Sir Aldane, because the two friends planned to build a **bridge** that
would have ended the Vell fortune. The Vells then built the bridge themselves
and called it neutral. **The assassin codename "the Ferryman"** is a family
tradition. **Her plan:** (1) a staged attack on the hostages, dressed up to
blame both sides; (2) if that fails, steal the Heart and frame the wizards;
(3) at the Midwinter ceremony, break the Heart's reliquary seals and let the
dragonfire burn the treaty hall, so each side blames the other.

## Rules in play (from the story bible)
- **Life-Spark:** spells burn the caster's life. Big spells cost years.
- **The Heart-Thread mark** **(new visual):** a thin red line winding around
  both their left wrists. **The number of turns is their remaining shared
  life.** Big spells unwind a turn, and small spells fray it.
- **What the thread really is** (Emrys knows; Maren learns in Ch 20): Maren
  was dying, so Emrys tied her life to **his**. They live on **his** remaining
  years, about two or three, which is 9 turns. Cutting the thread with the
  Heart's fire returns Maren to her own full life (her wound has healed), and
  Emrys keeps only what's left of his.
- **Oath-steel** cuts spells while the oath is kept. **A broken oath kills the
  blade** until a truer oath is sworn. The first oath-steel was forged in
  dragonfire, so it can turn dragonfire aside **(confirmed)**.
- **The Heart:** the last dragon's heart, still beating. Its fire unmakes any
  spell. The knights carry it to the Span in an iron reliquary.
- **Thread count by chapter:** 9 after binding (Ch 5) → 8 (Ch 7) → 7 (Ch 13)
  → 6 (Ch 14) → 5 (Ch 22) → 2 (Ch 30) → cut (Ch 31).
- **Clock:** Midwinter dawn is **14 days** after the exchange. Part 1 covers
  days 1–4, Part 2 days 5–8, Part 3 days 9–11, Part 4 days 12–14.

## PART 1: THE BURNED MARCH
1. **Maren: "By Steel and By Dawn."** Pre-dawn drill at Dawnhold, reciting the
   oath. The Wall of the Broken, where dead blades of oath-breakers hang (shows
   the rule). She secretly keeps her father's dead blade. Sir Aldric names her
   the peace-hostage, promises knighthood at the summit, and hands her sealed
   orders: *"If the treaty fails, the wizard does not return to his Tower."* She
   burns the letter. She takes her father's blade, wrapped, and leaves for the
   Neutral Stone.
2. **Emrys: "The Ledger."** At the Tower of Hours, Emrys casts a dazzling spell
   for the masters and hides a new gray streak with a joke. Archmaster Ysolde
   shows him his Ledger page: the tower is owed 30 years of service, the flood spell at 11 paid only part of it, and he still owes about 18 years. He has 2–3 winters left, so he'll die owing. Her
   offer: steal the Heart at the summit, and his debt is wiped and he earns his
   Master's star. Her reason: the Heart could free every wizard from the
   Ledger. He remembers reading about a forbidden binding in the restricted
   archive (seed). He leaves for the Stone.
3. **Maren: "The Neutral Stone."** The exchange; the first meeting. Banter, and
   "Sir Grim" is born. Captain Dace (jovial) and six Span wardens. The Warden
   chose the March route "for secrecy." They pass a field of dead blades. Emrys
   casts a small warming spell, and a strand of hair grays. Maren judges him.
4. **Emrys: "Fire in Knight's Colors."** Night two. Masked attackers, some in
   knights' white and gold, one throwing wizard fire. The wardens die. Dace
   falls into a ravine. Maren fights brilliantly and cuts a fire spell in two.
   She takes a blade meant for Emrys and is dying. Emrys chooses the forbidden
   binding, and darkness falls.
5. **Maren: "One Life."** She wakes with the red mark (9 turns). Emrys explains
   the half-truth: "one life, shared." She's furious and points her sword at
   him, then realizes killing him kills her. The dead attackers wore knights'
   colors, carried wizard fire-flasks, and had Span silver in their purses.
   They decide to go on to the Span by Midwinter. Her rule: no spells unless
   she says so. *"That's my life you're spending."*
6. **Emrys: "What the March Remembers."** Old battle-wards (magic traps). Emrys
   reads them, and Maren cuts one. Teamwork. They trade their sides' versions
   of the war and argue: *their fights are the war.* Pursuers' horns sound
   behind them.
7. **Maren: "Hollin's Ford."** A wary village. Tamsin sings *The Knight and the
   Lantern Prince*: "they were friends." The assassins attack the village.
   Emrys casts a great shield to save the villagers, and the thread drops to 8.
   Maren feels a year fall out of her. She's angry, but she saw him do it for
   strangers.
8. **Emrys: "Span Silver."** A captured, dying assassin confesses: hired
   killers, paid in Span silver by "the Ferryman." They weren't knights or
   wizards. **(Part 1's question is closed; who hired them stays open.)**
   Tamsin gives Maren a ribbon. Maren almost says his name. The only way south
   in time is through the Hollowwood. Emrys hides the ache in his chest.

## PART 2: THE HOLLOWWOOD
9. **Maren: "The Wood That Remembers."** They enter. Echoes appear: knights and
   wizards training *together*. Maren's world tilts.
10. **Emrys: "The Hollowed."** The forest drinks magic, so casting costs more
    here. The Hollowed are echo-soldiers still fighting their last battle, and
    they attack anyone wearing colors. Emrys saves Maren with cleverness, not
    magic.
11. **Maren: "Dead Blades."** By the fire, they share wounds: her father, the
    flood. The gray in his hair isn't dye. **She says "Emrys" for the first
    time.**
12. **Emrys: "Concord Keep."** In the ruin, the strongest echo: Sir Aldane and
    Prince Caelan, friends, planning **a bridge across the Sunder**. A third
    figure whose face won't resolve. A fragment: "...the ferries..." In the
    tomb they find the "murder dagger," with a small boat mark under the hilt
    (a clue they can't read yet).
13. **Maren: "The Falcon."** A knights' falcon finds her. Sir Aldric writes:
    talks are failing, and *"Remember your orders."* She hides the letter, and
    Emrys sees her hide something. The thread drops to 7 after small spells.
    Both secrets get heavier.
14. **Emrys: "The Anchor."** The sealing spell's anchor, a great tree grown over
    the prince's tomb. The Hollowed swarm them. Emrys casts big to get them
    out, and the thread drops to 6. He collapses. **(The Milieu thread
    closes: they're out.)**
15. **Maren: "Carrying Him."** She carries him to shelter and tends him. A
    tender moment, the first spark. His gray streak has widened, and she
    starts to wonder what the thread really costs.
16. **Emrys: "Two Camps."** At the foot of the Greyspine Pass: knights' banners
    *and* tower banners. They walk into separate camps. Sir Aldric sees the red
    mark on Maren's wrist, and his face goes cold. Ysolde's eyes find Emrys.
    Hook.

## PART 3: THE BROKEN OATH
17. **Maren: "The Reliquary."** The knights' camp, and the Heart beating in iron.
    It's a wonder. Aldric questions her and learns of the thread. *"The Heart's
    fire can cut it. When the time comes, you'll be free to do your duty."*
    Pre-summit talks are failing: the tower wants the Heart shared. A storm is
    building.
18. **Emrys: "The Arithmetic."** Ysolde reads the thread and says it aloud:
    Maren lives on *his* few years. Cutting the thread gives her life back.
    Order: steal the Heart tonight. Emrys decides to do it, for Maren as much
    as for his orders.
19. **Maren: "The Stream Between the Camps."** A secret meeting at night. They
    nearly confess. He asks, "If you could cut it, would you?" She says yes,
    and they hurt each other. The storm breaks.
20. **Emrys: "Both Secrets."** Emrys goes for the Heart and finds Maren already
    in the reliquary tent (Aldric sent her). Both sets of orders come out. He
    tells her the truth about the years. **The bond breaks.**
21. **Maren: "The Ferryman."** Assassins strike in the storm. A masked leader
    takes the Heart, and Maren tears the mask: **Dace, alive.** He escapes.
    Emrys is found unconscious beside the empty reliquary.
22. **Emrys: "Trial at Dawn."** Emrys is accused, and the tower is furious.
    Aldric orders the "thief" executed, knowing it will kill Maren, and offers
    to restore her father's name. **Maren refuses and steps in front of
    Emrys.** **Her blade dies in her hand.** Chaos. Emrys casts to cover their
    escape, and the thread drops to 5.
23. **Maren: "Two Dead Blades."** A storm hut. Her father's blade and hers, a
    matched pair. She understands her father. Emrys's apology, and a quiet
    reconciliation. They resolve to follow the Heart to the Span.
24. **Emrys: "Over the Pass."** Hunted by both sides, they climb. At dawn they see
    the Span glittering across the Sunder, two days to Midwinter. Only they
    know Dace is alive. Hook.

## PART 4: THE BRIDGE OF DAWN
25. **Maren: "The City on the Bridge."** They slip into the Span in disguise.
    Wonder. Posters name them as traitors. A founder's statue reads *"Corvane
    Vell, the Ferryman, who built the Span,"* and the clue clicks.
26. **Emrys: "Corvane's Ledger."** In the Vell archive, Corvane's own 300-year-old
    account: he paid to murder Caelan and frame Aldane to stop the bridge.
    Plus Isaura's recent letters to "the Ferryman." **The truth of the war.**
27. **Maren: "The Warden."** Isaura catches them, gracious and terrifying. Her
    reason: she holds the scales. Her plan for the dawn ceremony. They're
    captured; Maren's dead blade is taken, and Emrys is cuffed in oath-steel.
28. **Emrys: "Under the Bridge."** Cells hang over the Sunder. Emrys knows the
    bridge's old service ways from the archive, and Maren picks the lock. Dace
    comes to kill them quietly, and they fight on girders above the chasm.
    Maren beats Dace with her father's dead blade used as plain steel, and Dace
    falls into the Sunder for real.
29. **Maren: "My Own Oath."** The Midwinter dawn ceremony. Isaura breaks the
    reliquary seals, and dragonfire erupts. **Maren swears her own oath, and
    her blade blazes alive.** She turns the dragonfire up through the roof: a
    pillar of fire at dawn.
30. **Emrys: "The Echo."** Emrys casts the great echo-spell he learned in the
    Hollowwood, showing everyone Corvane's crime and Isaura's own words. The
    thread drops to 2. Isaura is exposed and seized. Aldric and Ysolde stand
    down.
31. **Maren: "Cutting the Thread."** The Heart's fire, drawn by Aldric and Ysolde
    *together*, cuts the thread. Maren's own life returns. Emrys keeps about a
    year or two, if he never casts again. Aldric confesses he gave the order
    her father refused. Ysolde tears out Emrys's Ledger page. Maren: "You don't
    get to decide alone anymore."
32. **Emrys: "Dawn."** The treaty is sworn and the Bridgewatch is founded:
    knights and wizards together, with Maren and Emrys as its first members.
    Maren speaks her own oath at dawn, mirroring Ch 1. Emrys chooses not to
    cast, takes her hand, and laughs for real. **Season 2 hook:** the Heart
    beats faster, and far below in the Sunder, something answers.
