# AI Use Log & Human Review Checklist

**How we write:** AI drafts, and you review and edit before anything goes into
the book. This log does two jobs:
1. **Publishing:** it reminds you to tick **"AI-generated"** on KDP for any
   book containing these drafts.
2. **Ownership:** it records your human edits. Copyright covers what *you*
   add and change, not the raw AI text, so the more you rewrite (and note
   here), the more of the book is yours.

---

## Human review checklist (do this for every AI draft)

- [ ] **Read it aloud,** or play it on the read-aloud page. Does it sound like
      people talking, or like a machine?
- [ ] **Check it against the story bible:** names, magic rules (Life-Spark,
      oath-steel, the Heart-Thread), who knows what, and when.
- [ ] **Cut anything generic:** stock phrases, repeated words, characters who
      "smile," "nod," and "sigh" too much (Lesson 8).
- [ ] **Make it yours:** change at least a few words or lines so it sounds
      like *your* book. Bigger rewrites mean more ownership.
- [ ] **Log what you changed** in the table below, even small things.
- [ ] **Approve it:** mark the entry ✅ when you're happy.
